# Exchange home

Mode: Operate. First-time hackathon judges must understand and use a live Solana devnet market for GPU-hour index tokens without hunting through product copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calibrated GPU exchange: the live market register, selected contract, real history, and order ticket form one instrument instead of a dashboard of detached cards.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve the established Calibrated GPU Ledger—deep graphite sheets, cool charcoal rows, calibrated hairlines, dusty-periwinkle registration marks, square controls, Onest with Azeret Mono for measurement, and the existing pixel GPU plates.

STORY: Within seconds, a visitor reads that Compute Market turns independent GPU-hour benchmarks into tradable Solana index tokens, sees five real Ornn markets, selects a GPU, understands the chart and contract state, and can connect a wallet to claim devnet USDC and buy or redeem the index. Launching and browsing community markets remain available as a secondary workflow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim masthead and one concise product sentence lead directly into a three-column exchange: compact market register on the left, selected GPU image plus real Ornn history in the center, and a persistent buy/sell ticket on the right. Deployment status and the selected feed address are visible but quiet. The market itself proves the product; there is no generic hero or fake aggregate metric.

FORM: Extend seed 72b87859 and the approved calibrated-ledger composition into an operate surface. Signature interaction: selecting a GPU moves the periwinkle registration mark, swaps its pixel hardware plate, redraws its verified price history, and retargets the trade ticket without shifting the terminal frame.

FINISH: Desktop and mobile must expose working real-data, loading, empty, wallet-disconnected, transaction-pending, success, and recoverable-error states. The build ends only after protocol smoke tests, production build, browser QA, Impeccable detection/review, and documented design changes.

## Constraints

- Use only verified Ornn prices/history and fetched on-chain state; never invent volume, change, liquidity, holder count, or activity.
- Label devnet and test USDC plainly. Transactions must target the deployed program, not a simulation.
- Keep product explanation to one precise sentence plus compact mechanism copy; deep methodology and address detail stay secondary.
- Use the existing raster GPU plates with provenance and pixelated rendering; do not substitute decorative crypto imagery.
- Preserve the docs route and the established graphite/periwinkle brand system.

## Unresolved decisions

None. The user explicitly fixed the information density, exchange hierarchy, real-data requirement, GPU detail/chart behavior, and visual theme.
