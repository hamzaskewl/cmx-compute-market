# Exchange home

Mode: Operate. Visitors inspect current Ornn GPU-hour indexes and evaluate the Solana market mechanism. The page must not imply activity or deployment that cannot be verified.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Treat each GPU index as a calibrated instrument plate, not a crypto-card collectible. The page refuses the familiar dark neon trading dashboard and the incumbent oversized hardware gallery.

OWN-WORLD: Warm near-white stock, graphite ink, calibrated hairlines, cobalt-violet registration marks, square controls, compact voxel GPU stamps, and one confident grotesque with a restrained measurement face for prices.

STORY: A visitor immediately sees what Compute Market is, reads the latest available Ornn references, understands that the protocol uses a reserve-backed mint/redeem mechanism, and follows source or program documentation without encountering simulated activity.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim registered masthead sits above a two-column plate. The proposition and source action occupy the left; a live five-row quote register and small voxel GPU assembly occupy the right. The price data is the hero, with no fake aggregate metric.

FORM: Calibrated GPU benchmark plate, fourth in the grounded direction list; seed key 72b87859. Signature interaction: selecting an index aligns its registration mark and reveals the corresponding compact specification line without moving the quote grid.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

- Use only real Ornn values and dates returned by the live feed.
- Do not render fake tokens, balances, launches, movement percentages, volume, liquidity, or chain status.
- Keep Solana program economics factual and label them as protocol design rather than live performance.
- Use code-native geometry for the GPU marks; no shipping raster is required.
